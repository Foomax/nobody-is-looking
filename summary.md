## 1. Repo purpose, and how the deliverable differs from it

First commit (`096cac0`):

> Initial commit. This repo aims to be a meta-analysis of all online empirical AI Safety work.

Original task prompt:

> open ~/alignment-forum-scrape and ~/scrape-lesswrong and neel-nandas-chris-olah, in particular
> the readme.md in each. Find common features in the document, patterns in the differences, and
> OUTLIERS. Brainstorm further research ideas for an LLM to execute. Our goal is to write a
> document readable by other LLMs: readme.md. For the .json — Section 1. An analysis … Section 2.
> A synthesis & a brainstorm … using max thinking tokens, write further research recommendations —
> again, written for LLMs.

**`corpus-analysis.md` is a meta-analysis of the three *scrapes* — overlap, recall, label hygiene,
instrument artifacts. `p3/findings.md` is the meta-analysis of the *research* — what the 741
posts found — and is the deliverable the first commit asked for.** Read `p3/findings.md` §0
before quoting it: the extraction instrument is an LLM judge that no human has validated (P4).

Since then, `replication/` turned `findings.md` into a run queue for the RTX 3090 in this machine:
87 experiments with a pinned commit, an entrypoint, a target number and a budget, each with its
own Opus prompt. None has been run.

## 8. Conventions

- **Tiers.** `[MEASURED]` = a number `meta.py` emits into `numbers.json`. A judgment is not
  `[MEASURED]`; "directly measurable" is not measured. `[INFERRED]` / `[SPECULATIVE]` /
  `[UNRESOLVED]` as defined at the top of `corpus-analysis.md`.
- **Change the script, not the prose** — now enforced by `test_numbers.py`.
- **Every rate carries n and a Wilson interval; every median a bootstrap interval.**
- **Never compare a partial period to a full one.** 2024H2 is 129 days, 2026H2 is 56, full
  periods are 181–184. Per-30-day rates are in `numbers.json` under `period.*_per30d`.
- **Do not re-run the source scrapes.** Karma is an age-confounded snapshot and several
  comparisons depend on both corpora sharing one.
- **Subagents are pre-approved** (user's global CLAUDE.md) for fan-out work and should not be
  asked about. P3 is the natural place; this analysis was done inline because the join needs one
  context.
- `prompts.md` P0 is the ground-rules block to prepend to any further work here.
