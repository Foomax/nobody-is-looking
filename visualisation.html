01 · Where the attempts went

Everything that failed, failed before the science.

Sixteen of thirty-six never produced a number. Not one of them failed on the claim being tested: they failed on packaging, missing files, or hardware. The single drop that matters is the third bar — fourteen experiments installed cleanly and still could not be made to run.

02 · The sixteen that never ran

“Broken environment” turned out to be five different things.

Eight of these rows were re-attempted with the environment reconstructed at the post's own date and uncommitted files regenerated by the authors' own code. Seven then produced a measurement, and none of them contradicted its author. In none of the eight did the first error message name the real cause: underneath were uncommitted artefacts, a lockfile that never resolved, code broken at publication, and one bug in our own harness.

03 · What a reproduction depends on

Seeds move verdicts. Library versions do not.

Fifteen further runs changed exactly one thing at a time against a row that had already reproduced. The result that survived every software change did not survive a change of random seed — and the field reports a seed count in 13.7% of posts.

04 · The corpus this came from

The form of an empirical report has converged. Its statistics have not.

All 741 posts were read by an LLM against a fixed rubric before any code was run. Nine in ten name a comparison condition and admit a limitation; fewer than one in five say how much of the result could be noise. No post satisfies all six design markers.

06 · Method and limits

Protocol. Pin the author's commit; one virtual environment per experiment; fix the environment only, logging every fix; no hosted-model APIs and no accounts; pre-register the pass/fail rule; record a verdict whether or not it succeeds. Runs that deliberately change the model, the seed set, the library epoch or the dataset are filed as variations and never enter the reproduction rate.

What “reproduced” means here. Exact = recomputed from source and matched to the stated precision. Recompute = re-ran the author's analysis over their committed artefacts — weaker, and labelled as such. A three-decimal match obtained with the author's own seed is a statement about the random number generator, not about the effect.

Limits. The sample is minute-class experiments plus three protocol runs; the hours-class test is n=2, so the pattern is consistent at scale, not confirmed. One machine, one operator: four failures are this card's limits rather than universal ones. The corpus-level rates in section 04 come from an LLM judge that no human has validated — the contrasts are more robust than the levels.

Not done. Two rows stay blocked because they need a login this audit will not create. Three more are simply long: six to eight GPU-hours each, queued rather than abandoned.
